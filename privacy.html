SylviLive Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026.

SylviLive is a personal application used by one person, its owner, to manage his own YouTube live broadcasts through the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed: with the owner's authorization, SylviLive accesses the owner's own YouTube channel and live broadcast metadata (such as broadcast titles, status, and stream settings) using the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube, solely to create, schedule, and manage the owner's broadcasts.

Data storage: OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's personal computer. SylviLive does not run a public server, does not collect data from any other person, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Data retention and deletion: the owner can revoke SylviLive's access at any time from his Google account's third-party access page, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.
